At **MmiriOma**, we respect your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect when you create an account, request water, receive a delivery, pay, contact us or deliver for us as a rider or driver; why we use it; who receives it; how long we keep it; and how you can exercise your rights. We aim to handle personal data in accordance with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023** and other applicable laws.

MmiriOma is running as a **small pilot**. This notice describes the service as it works during the pilot. When the service changes, we will update this notice first (section 15).

## 3. Why do we use personal data?

We use personal information to create and protect accounts; verify phone numbers; agree prices and delivery times; arrange water supply, dispatch and delivery; send messages about your order; record cash payments; pay refunds; confirm delivery; investigate complaints and incidents; prevent misuse; keep the service secure; and comply with the law.

Depending on the activity, our lawful basis may be performing or preparing a contract with you, complying with legal obligations, our legitimate interests where appropriate, or your consent where the law requires it. We do not send promotional messages.

## 4. Phone verification and service messages

You must verify your mobile number before your first order. We send a 6-digit code by SMS; it works for **5 minutes** and is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, so we cannot read it back. We also send SMS messages about your order, such as when it is confirmed, when the driver sets off, and your delivery code, and we show order updates as messages in the app. We do not sell or share telephone numbers with advertisers.

## 5. Addresses and location data

We use the address, landmark and instructions you type to decide whether we can deliver, agree the price and find you. The app does not ask for your phone's location. The only location we record is the optional GPS position of the **driver's** phone at arrival and at delivery, with the driver's consent (section 2). We do not track you or show live vehicle locations.

## 6. Payments, refunds and delivery evidence

During the pilot you **pay in cash on delivery**, after the water has been unloaded. The app does not take card, online or bank-transfer payments. We record the amount due, the cash collected, and its handover from the driver to our staff.

Refunds are decided and paid by our administrator outside the app. If a refund is paid by bank transfer, we ask for the account details needed for that transfer and use them only for that refund.

When the driver records that unloading is complete, we send a 6-digit **delivery code** by SMS to the order's contact number. It works for **10 minutes** and is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form. You give the code to the driver to confirm you received the water. If you cannot receive the code, our administrator may approve other evidence instead, such as your acknowledgment, the delivery time and the driver's report, and records who approved it and why. The app takes no photographs. **Confirming that water was received is separate from confirming that it was paid for.**

## 7. Who receives your information?

We share only what is needed to run the service:

- **The driver assigned to your order** sees the contact's first name and last initial, phone number, delivery address, landmark, instructions and quantity, and only while they are assigned to the delivery.
- **Our authorized staff** (administrators and dispatchers) see order and account details, including riders' and drivers' vehicle and approval details, to confirm, dispatch and support orders and to approve riders and drivers.
- **Termii**, our SMS provider, receives your phone number and the text of each message we send you.
- **Hostinger** (HOSTINGER Global S.à r.l., Luxembourg), our hosting provider, runs the server and stores the service's data and backups in its data centre in Paris, France.
- **Backblaze** (Backblaze, Inc., United States) stores an off-site copy of our backups in its data centre in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.

The app does not give your details to water suppliers or advertisers. We may disclose data when required by law. Because our hosting provider stores the service's data in France and our backup provider stores copies of it in the Netherlands, your personal information is processed outside Nigeria; we assess and apply the safeguards required by Nigerian data-protection law for such transfers, and do the same for any other provider that processes personal data outside Nigeria.

## 8. How long do we keep information?

We keep information only as long as set out below. Our staff review stored data **every month** and delete or anonymize what has reached the end of its period; the period in the table is the earliest it becomes due, so it may be removed at the first monthly review after that. We may keep a limited record longer when justified by law, an active dispute, a fraud investigation or a documented legal hold (section 10). Deletion may mean removing information or irreversibly anonymizing it.

| Data category | How long we keep it | What happens afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| Phone verification codes | Usable for 5 minutes | The scrambled code is deleted at the next monthly review; the record of when your number was verified or changed is kept with your account |
| Delivery codes | Usable for 10 minutes | The scrambled code is deleted at the next monthly review; the record that delivery was confirmed is kept with the order |
| Accounts never verified | 30 days without verification | The account is deleted. If our records of important changes must keep a reference to it (for example, the account changed its password), it is anonymized and closed instead: its name and phone number are removed and it can no longer be used |
| Your account and profile | While your account is open and needed for the service | Deleted or anonymized after a verified deletion request (section 9), subject to the exceptions in section 10 |
| Saved addresses | While your account is open | Deleted when your account is closed |
| Messages in the app | As long as the order they are about | Deleted when your account is closed |
| Order and delivery records, including delivery addresses | 3 years after the order is completed | Personal details deleted or anonymized; the payment subset is kept as below |
| Feedback on an order | As long as the order record (3 years after the order is completed) | Your message is removed when the order's personal details are deleted or anonymized, or when your account is closed; the rating and answers stay with the anonymized order, as they no longer identify you |
| Driver GPS points | 90 days after the delivery | Deleted, unless needed for an open complaint or dispute |
| Cash payment and refund records, including refund bank details | At least 6 years, or longer where Nigerian accounting or tax law requires | Securely deleted or anonymized when that obligation ends |

| Sign-in sessions | A session lasts up to 7 days | Ended sessions are deleted at the next monthly review |

## 9. How can you request access, correction or deletion?

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by calling **+234 813 377 4902** or emailing **[olivetgrail@gmail.com](mailto:olivetgrail@gmail.com)**. Before we disclose or change anything, we confirm the request comes from you, normally by calling back the phone number on your account. For deletion, you can also use "Delete my account" in the customer app; we still call you back to confirm it is you.

- We acknowledge a request within **3 working days**.
- We aim to complete a straightforward request within **14 calendar days**, and always within any deadline the law sets.
- We tell you if some records must be kept (for example cash payment records, an open refund or an unresolved dispute), and we restrict their use.

For a deletion request, we first check for open orders, cash not yet settled, refunds and disputes. We then close your account, which signs you out everywhere, delete or anonymize your profile and the personal details on your orders, keep only the records we must keep, and tell you the outcome.

## 10. Exceptions and backups

Records may be kept beyond the periods in section 8 for documented legal obligations, regulatory matters, litigation, fraud investigations or unresolved disputes. Access to these records is limited, and each exception is reviewed when its purpose ends.

Deleting information from the live service does not immediately remove it from existing encrypted backups or from our SMS provider's records. Backups expire within 30 days. If we ever restore a backup, we apply every deletion request made since that backup was taken before the service is used again.

## 11. Security

We protect your information with HTTPS, a secure sign-in cookie, access limited to the staff and driver who need it, scrambled (hashed) passwords and codes, and records of important changes made by staff. No digital system can guarantee absolute security. Where a security incident triggers a legal notification duty, we will follow the applicable requirements.

## 12. Your privacy rights

Subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and lawful exceptions, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent where processing depends on it; and exercise any applicable data-portability rights. You can contact us using the details in section 16. You may also complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** at [ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng/).

## 13. Cookies and sessions

We use one necessary cookie to keep you signed in. It cannot be read by scripts on the page and lasts up to 7 days, or until you sign out. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, and the app does not store your personal details in your browser.

## 14. Children's privacy

Customer accounts are for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly let children create ordering accounts. If we learn that a child's personal information has been collected without an appropriate legal basis, we will delete it or take other necessary corrective action.

## 15. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our service, providers or data practices change, and before the change takes effect where we can. The current version and its date are shown at the top. We will give additional notice where the law requires it.
